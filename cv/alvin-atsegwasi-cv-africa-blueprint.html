Profile

A builder who learned his trade where plans are tested hardest. For ten years Alvin turned a British Army unit's paper entitlement into equipment that arrived in the right condition, at the right time, and accounted for down to the last item.

He served a decade with the Royal Electrical and Mechanical Engineers (REME), leaving as Acting Technical Quartermaster Sergeant, the post responsible for a unit's entire equipment holding. For about nine years he has led The Crownshield Group in Abuja, bringing the same discipline to Nigerian security and defence procurement. He also leads ventures in UK security, veterans' welfare and education. His contribution to Africa's builders is simple: a blueprint is only as good as the accountability that carries it into the field.
